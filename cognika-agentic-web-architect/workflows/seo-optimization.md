# Workflow: SEO optimization

## Inputs
A completed audit or a clearly scoped SEO issue, plus permission to edit the relevant code/content.

## Procedure

1. Confirm target pages and business goals.
2. Prioritize crawl/indexing blockers and broken journeys.
3. Fix technical foundations: redirects, canonicals, robots, sitemap, rendering, and internal links.
4. Improve titles, descriptions, headings, and content clarity without keyword stuffing.
5. Add or correct structured data only where supported by visible page content.
6. Improve image metadata and performance where relevant.
7. Check mobile and accessibility regressions.
8. Validate representative URLs and templates.
9. Document changed files, before/after behavior, test evidence, and rollback.

## Completion criteria

- Changes are scoped and reversible.
- Canonical, sitemap, robots, and internal-link signals are consistent.
- No unsupported claims or fabricated structured data were added.
- Tests are recorded in the implementation report.
